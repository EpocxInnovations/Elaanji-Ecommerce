# Elaanji User Documentation

## Introduction
Welcome to Elaanji, a premium e-commerce platform for garments and sarees. This documentation provides a comprehensive guide on how to navigate and use the features available for both customers and store administrators.

---

## 1. Customer Guide

### 1.1 Getting Started
- **Registration & Login**: Create an account using your email address and a secure password. If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to securely reset it.
- **Navigation**: Browse through various categories such as Kanjivaram, Banarasi, Cotton, and Designer sarees using the main navigation bar.

### 1.2 Shopping Experience
- **Product Catalog**: Explore our extensive collection of sarees. Use search and filtering options to find exactly what you're looking for.
- **Product Details**: Click on any product to view detailed descriptions, multiple high-quality images, available variants (e.g., colors), and real-time stock status.
- **Cart & Wishlist**: 
  - Add products to your **Cart** to proceed with a purchase.
  - Save items to your **Wishlist** if you plan to buy them later.

### 1.3 Checkout & Orders
- **Address Management**: Save multiple shipping and billing addresses in your profile for a seamless and quick checkout process.
- **Coupons & Discounts**: Apply valid promotional codes during checkout to avail of special discounts.
- **Payment Options**: Securely complete your purchase using multiple payment options including Razorpay and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **Order Tracking**: View your complete order history, track the status of current orders, and download invoices from the "My Profile" section.

---

## 2. Administrator Guide

### 2.1 Accessing the Admin Panel
Administrators can access the backend dashboard by logging in with their designated admin credentials. The dashboard provides full control over the store's operations and content.

### 2.2 Dashboard & Analytics
- **Overview**: View critical Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as total sales, active orders, and inventory alerts at a glance.
- **Reports**: Generate and analyze sales and inventory reports to make informed business decisions.

### 2.3 Catalog & Inventory Management
- **Products**: Create new products, update existing listings, and manage product variants (like colors and sizes). Include detailed descriptions and manage product images.
- **Categories**: Create and organize hierarchical product categories.
- **Inventory**: Monitor stock levels across all products. Update quantities manually as new stock arrives.

### 2.4 Order & Fulfillment Management
- **Orders**: View and manage all customer orders. Update order statuses through the fulfillment lifecycle (e.g., Processing, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled).
- **Refunds**: Process and track customer refund requests for returned or cancelled items.

### 2.5 Customer & Marketing Management
- **Customers**: View customer profiles, their complete order histories, and manage user accounts.
- **Coupons**: Create, edit, and manage promotional discount codes. Set rules such as usage limits, minimum spend, and expiration dates.
- **Reviews**: Moderate and manage product reviews submitted by customers.
- **Banners & Content**: Update promotional banners and manage homepage content to highlight new arrivals, seasonal collections, or special offers.
- **Settings**: Configure application settings and SEO parameters.
